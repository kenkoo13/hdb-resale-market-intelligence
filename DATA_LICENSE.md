## Publisher's notes on the data (summarised)

1. The approximate floor area includes any recess area purchased, space-adding items under HDB upgrading programmes, roof terraces, and so on.
2. The transactions exclude resales that may not reflect full market price, such as resale between relatives and resale of part shares.
3. Resale prices should be taken as indicative only, because agreed prices depend on many factors.
